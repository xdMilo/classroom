Privacy Policy

updated jan 24 2026

We're not here to spy on you. This policy explains what data we actually collect (spoiler: not much) and what we do with it.

The Short Version: We collect basically nothing. Your settings live on your device, we use Google ads to keep the lights on, and Firebase counts how many people are online. That's it. We don't sell anything.

1. What We Actually Collect

Automatic Stuff

When you visit, we automatically see some basic tech info:

  • What browser you're using
  • Your operating system and screen size
  • Which pages you look at
  • Your IP address (but it's anonymized)

This is just standard web stuff that every site sees.

What's Saved On Your Device

Your browser saves your Aether preferences locally:

  • Your theme choice
  • Tab cloaking settings
  • Recently played games
  • Panic button settings
  • Whether you want the snow effect on or off

This stuff never leaves your device. It's all stored in your browser's local storage. You can nuke it anytime through browser settings or the "Clear Cache" button.

Firebase Counter

We use Firebase to show the "X users online" thing on the homepage. That's literally all it does - counts active sessions anonymously.

What We DON'T Collect

Just to be clear:

  • No names, emails, or personal info (there's no accounts anyway)
  • No passwords or payment info (the site is free)
  • No tracking what sites you visit through the seearch
  • No detailed browsing history

2. How We Use This Info

The minimal data we collect helps us:

  • Keep the site running
  • Make sure it works on different browsers
  • Show how many people are currently online
  • Fix bugs when they pop up
  • Display ads (that's how we pay for hosting)

3. Third-Party Services

Ad

We use Google ads. They track stuff to show relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.

More info: How Google uses data

Firebase

Firebase handles the online user counter. It's run by Google. Their privacy policy: Firebase Privacy

Games & Apps

Third-party games and apps have their own privacy policies. We don't control those.

4. Search Privacy

The search feature:

  • We don't log what sites you visit DOESNT MEAN YOUR SCHOOL CANT
  • We don't store any data from sites you access
  • Your search usage isn't tied to any identifier

Heads up: Your school/work network can still potentially see your traffic depending on their setup. The search feature hides it from the sites you visit, not necessarily from your network admin.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Here's what tracks you:

  • Local Storage: Saves your settings on your device forever (until you clear it)
  • Google Ads Cookies: For showing ads
  • Firebase: For counting users during your session

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some stuff might break.

6. Security

We try to keep things secure with HTTPS and minimal data collection. But nothing on the internet is 100% secure, so keep that in mind.

7. Kids

If you're under 13, you shouldn't be here. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you're 13-17, get your parents' permission before using the site.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • See your local data through browser dev tools
  • Delete everything with the "Clear Cache" button in settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads through Google
  • Block analytics with browser extensions

Since most data lives on your device anyway, you've got full control.

9. How Long We Keep Stuff

  • Local Storage: Forever, until you delete it
  • Firebase Stats: Whatever Firebase's default retention is
  • Server Logs: 30 days max for security purposes

10. International Use

The site is hosted in the US. If you're accessing from somewhere else, your data might pass through US servers. By using Aether, you're cool with that.

11. Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. We don't really respond to it since we're barely tracking anything anyway.

12. Policy Changes

This policy might get updated. Check back occasionally. The "Last Updated" date at the top tells you when we last changed something.

13. California Residents

CCPA gives you extra rights if you're in California. We don't sell personal info, so most of this doesn't apply. But you've got the right to know what we collect (covered above) and request deletion (use the Clear Cache button).

14. European Users

GDPR gives you rights like accessing your data, deleting it, and opting out. Most of your data is on your device, so you already have control. For the rest, use the features built into the site.

15. Questions?

Got questions? Use the feedback options on the site.
